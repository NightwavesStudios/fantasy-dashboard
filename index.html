How these rankings work
Teams are ranked by a blend of two things. Roster value comes from FantasyCalc trade values for a 10 team Superflex PPR league, added up for everyone on the roster. PPG is that team’s average points per game from ESPN after real games are played. Before scores start, rankings are pure roster value. Once games are in, PPG gets more weight as the season goes on (a little after week 1, a lot more by midseason). The chips under each team show rank at QB, RB, WR, TE, and BN. BN is the 6 lowest FantasyCalc values on the roster (after dropping 1 or 2 lowest if the team has IR extras over 14 players).
