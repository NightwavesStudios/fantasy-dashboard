How power rankings work

League-settings driven. Team count, starters, FLEX, and Superflex are read from your host — nothing assumes a fixed 10- or 12-team template.

What is VOR?

Value Over Replacement is surplus market value above a replacement-level player at the same position:

VORᵢ = max(0, Vᵢ − RVpos(i))

Players at or below replacement contribute 0.

Two external baselines (full FantasyCalc pool)

Neither baseline is “whoever is on your league’s benches.” Both come from the format’s full player pool and your starting demand (including optimized FLEX / Superflex).

  1. Fill all league starting slots with the highest-value legal pool players (pure slots → SF → FLEX).
  2. Starter replacement (RVstarter) — first player at that position outside starting demand. Answers: “how much better is this starter than the next guy who could take his job?”
  3. Waiver / depth bar (RVwaiver) — first player after starts plus one quality depth slot per team. Answers: “how much useful bench talent is above what you could stream?” Always ≤ starter RV.

Starter VORᵢ = max(0, Vᵢ − RV_starter)
Depth valueᵢ = max(0, Vᵢ − RV_waiver)

Starter VOR

Per team, legal lineup that maximizes Σ starter VOR. That sum is Starter VOR.

Depth VOR

Players outside that lineup use depth value (waiver bar), with diminishing returns by position: 1.0, 0.70, 0.50, 0.35, 0.25…. Bench talent above streamers still counts — it is not zeroed just because it sits under starter RV.

Positional VOR columns

Sum of starter-baseline VOR for every rostered player at that position (same units as Starter VOR).

Scoring & record

  • Avg / Med / Most / Least from weekly scores when ≥2 weeks exist. Most/Least are display-only (not in Power).
  • Record uses (wins + 1) / (games + 2).

Power score

score = clamp(50 + 50 × (x − med) / max(|max−med|, |min−med|), 0, 100) per component, then:

Starter VOR40% Depth VOR20% Average scored15% Median scored10% Record15%

Raw market totals are never in the formula.

How team needs work

Same VOR and replacement baseline as power rankings — so a “Need” here means the same kind of surplus gap the rankings use.

Shared VOR definition

Uses the same starter replacement baseline as power rankings: full FantasyCalc pool + league starting demand (FLEX / Superflex optimized).

VOR = max(0, value − RVstarter)

(Depth in rankings uses a separate, lower waiver bar so quality bench still scores there.)

What each card shows

For every team and position (QB / RB / WR / TE):

  • Positional VOR — sum of starter-baseline VOR for all rostered players at that position
  • % vs median — vs the league median at that position
  • Need / Avg / Strong — about −12% / near median / +12%

How to read it

  • Need — well below league median surplus; upgrading here moves the needle most
  • Avg — roughly in line with the middle of the league
  • Strong — clear surplus; often a trade-from spot

A zero means every player there is at or below starter replacement — not “no players,” but no surplus under that bar.

Why this matches rankings

Same starter RV and positional VOR columns as the rankings table. Needs is the per-team diagnostic view of that surplus map.
